# Turret engagement simulation

Generated by `engage.py`. A drone dives 20° at a point on the building at 30 m/s (108 km/h) unless stated; it is first seen 60 m out, from a random direction, so the head may have to turn up to 180°. Each cell is the share of 1000 engagements that end in a catch. Light launcher (2 m net, 36 J) unless stated.

Baseline: camera + 0.5 m ranging, 30 measurements a second with 80 ms delay, head turning 180°/s, 40 ms from fire command to the net leaving, net thrown within 1.5° and 4 % of its nominal speed.

## Tracking head v0: two fixed cameras as a stereo pair, hobby bus servos

Two identical cameras 0.4 m apart at 1280 x 720 give range from the difference between the two pictures; the error grows with distance squared (about 2.3 m at 60 m, 0.6 m at 30 m, 0.14 m at 15 m). Drone seen at 45 m, head turning 250°/s, 30 measurements a second with 50 ms delay, light launcher.
