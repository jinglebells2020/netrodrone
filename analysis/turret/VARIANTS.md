# Launcher variants

Generated by `launch_variants.py`. Every variant throws the same 4 m² of net with the same 80 g of weights at 30 m/s (36 J), fired 20° upward. 'Catch half-width' is how far off the aim line a hovering 0.25 m drone can be and still be caught. It is tried in three directions around the aim line and the worst is shown, because a net reaches further toward its corners than toward the middle of its sides.

Pushing the net out costs about 4 J more (the 11 g net leaving at 26 m/s), on top of the 36 J in the weights. The model has no friction between the net and the cup and no line-on-line contact, so it cannot show a net tangling; a twisted launch is where that would matter most.
