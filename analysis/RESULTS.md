## What breaks it, what doesn't (M120, 8 g weights)

| change from the design | weight speed (m/s) | change |
|---|---|---|
| design point (check valve + 1 mm bleed, O-rings, 4 mm nozzle) | 9.3 | +0 % |
| **no check valve** (system sealed between shots) | 0.0 | **weights do not leave the barrels** |
| no check valve, but a vented AEG piston head (~2 mm2) | 7.0 | -24 % |
